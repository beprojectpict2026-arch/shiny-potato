The city transport department has started replacing diesel buses with electric buses. Each electric bus runs on a large battery that is charged overnight at the depot. Electric buses produce no smoke on the road, so the air in busy areas is expected to become cleaner. Passengers also notice that the new buses are much quieter than the old ones. Charging stations are being built at four depots, and each station can charge twenty buses at the same time. Running costs of an electric bus are lower because electricity is cheaper than diesel and the motor has fewer parts to repair. Officials say the savings will help to keep ticket prices stable. Drivers are being trained to handle the new buses and to look after the batteries.
