Online learning has become a common way to study after schools and colleges started using video lessons. Students can watch recorded lectures at any time and repeat the parts they did not understand. Teachers share notes, assignments and quizzes on a single platform, which saves paper and time. A stable internet connection is the biggest problem for students in remote areas. Some students find it hard to stay focused when they study alone at home. Many colleges now mix classroom teaching with online lessons to get the benefits of both. Good online courses use short videos, regular practice questions and quick feedback to keep students interested.
